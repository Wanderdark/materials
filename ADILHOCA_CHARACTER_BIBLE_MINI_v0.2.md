# ADILHOCA CHARACTER BIBLE MINI v0.2

Purpose: compact source-of-truth for new chats.

## Global Prompt Rules

-   Use the SAME canonical student from the AdilHoca educational
    universe.
-   Preserve face, hairstyle, hair color, eye color, age, body
    proportions, skin tone, and identity.
-   Premium casual educational game asset; child-friendly; smooth
    rounded shapes; soft shading; clean outlines; vibrant colors;
    production-ready; --ar 1:1.
-   Avoid identical camera-facing smiles. Prefer natural expressions.
-   Characters usually look at the activity, object or teammate, not the
    camera.
