# AdilHoca Character Growth Concept (Growing With You)

## Vision

Instead of creating completely different illustrations for Grades 5, 6,
7 and 8, the same canonical AdilHoca students gradually grow together
with the learners.

The objective is **continuity**, not redesign.

A student who meets Olivia, Mia, Lucas or Noah in Grade 5 should
instantly recognize the same character in Grade 7.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Core Philosophy

Students don't only learn English.

They also build a relationship with recurring characters.

As real students grow older each school year, the AdilHoca students grow
with them.

This creates emotional continuity and a stronger educational universe.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Age Progression

  Grade     Approximate Age
  --------- -----------------
  Grade 5   10--11
  Grade 6   11--12
  Grade 7   12--13
  Grade 8   13--14

Growth should always be subtle and believable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Character Rules

## Never Change

-   Core facial identity
-   Eye color
-   Personality
-   Overall hairstyle identity (natural evolution is acceptable)
-   Character role

## May Naturally Change

-   Height
-   Limb proportions
-   Slight facial maturity
-   Clothing
-   Body language
-   Interests becoming more advanced
-   Confidence

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Clothing Rule

One of the strongest continuity signals is clothing progression.

Characters should NEVER wear the same outfit after two school years.

Grade 5: - brighter colors - simpler children's clothing

Grade 7: - fresh outfits - slightly more mature middle-school style -
still modest and child-friendly

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Story Progression

Do not simply recreate the same scene.

Instead, continue the story.

Example:

Grade 5

Olivia: "Come on, let's go inside the bookstore!"

Mia: Holding books, excited to enter.

↓

Grade 7

Olivia: Walking out while excitedly showing a newly purchased novel.

Mia: Carrying a reusable bookstore bag full of books.

The friendship remains identical. Only the maturity changes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Prompt Workflow

## Step 1

Generate the Grade 5 illustration.

Treat it as the canonical base image.

## Step 2

Use that Grade 5 illustration as the reference image.

Prompt:

-   Same girls
-   Same location
-   Same atmosphere
-   Same composition
-   Approximately two school years later
-   New clothing
-   Natural physical growth
-   Slightly more mature body language
-   Continue the story instead of repeating it

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Important Prompt Notes

Always include ideas such as:

-   "Maintain exactly the same facial identity."
-   "The viewer should instantly recognize them as the same girls."
-   "Do not create different characters."
-   "Use completely different outfits."
-   "The location should remain familiar."
-   "Continue the story naturally."

Avoid:

-   Grade labels
-   Before/After captions
-   Split-screen text
-   Obvious comparison graphics

The two illustrations should feel like photographs taken at the same
place two years apart.

# Why This Matters

Most educational resources simply repeat vocabulary.

This concept creates a living educational universe.

Students progress academically while the characters progress alongside
them.

The long-term goal is that learners feel:

"I grew up together with these characters."

This emotional continuity can become one of the defining features of the
AdilHoca educational universe.
